Common Questions

Before You Reach Out

How do I start a project with VOAD?+

The simplest way is to fill out the contact form on this page or call us directly at +91 81603 32950. Tell us a little about your project, the type of space, the city, a rough timeline, and what you are hoping to achieve. We will get back to you personally, usually within a day, to schedule an initial conversation. There is no obligation at that stage. We want to understand your brief before either side commits to anything.

Do you offer a free first consultation?+

Yes. The initial conversation is free and carries no obligation. We use it to understand what you are trying to build and whether we are the right fit for each other. If there is potential for a working relationship, we will outline what a formal engagement would look like, including scope, timeline, and fees. We will not pressure you into a decision during that first meeting.

Can you work on projects outside Rajkot?+

Yes. We are based in Rajkot and regularly work with clients across Gujarat, including Ahmedabad, Surat, Vadodara, Gandhinagar, Jamnagar, and Bhavnagar. We also take on architecture and interior design projects in Mumbai, Pune, and other cities across India. Ar. Vivek Bosmiya travels to site personally at key stages of every project. Geography does not limit what we can work on.

How long does a typical project take from first meeting to handover?+

It depends on the scope. A full residential interior design project typically runs 4 to 8 months from the start of design to handover. Architecture projects that include construction supervision can run 12 to 24 months depending on complexity and scale. Smaller renovation or fitout projects can be faster. We will give you a realistic timeline estimate once we understand your brief, and we build milestones into every project so you always know where things stand.

What is the minimum project size you accept?+

We do not have a rigid minimum. What we look for is a project where design genuinely matters to the client and where there is enough scope for us to do meaningful work. A single room redesign where the brief is interesting can be as rewarding as a full bungalow commission. If you are unsure whether your project is the right size for us, just reach out and ask. The conversation will make it clear.
