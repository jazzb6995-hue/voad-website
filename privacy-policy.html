Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2025

1. Who We Are

VOAD Architecture and Interiors is an architecture and interior design studio based at RK Trade Tower, A/612, 150 Feet Ring Road, Rajkot, Gujarat 360006, India. Telephone: +91 81603 32950. Website: www.voad.in.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name, email address, and phone number submitted through our contact form
  • Project details and preferences you share when enquiring about our services

We also collect limited technical information automatically when you visit our website, including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on pages (via Google Analytics).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiries about architecture or interior design services
  • Communicate with you about your project
  • Improve our website and understand how visitors use it

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics to understand visitor behaviour. Google Analytics sets cookies on your browser to collect data about your visit. This data is processed by Google in accordance with their privacy policy. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

5. Data Retention

We retain enquiry data for as long as necessary to respond to your project request and for a reasonable period thereafter in case of follow-up. Analytics data is retained for 26 months in accordance with Google Analytics defaults.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, to request correction of inaccurate data, or to request deletion of your data. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the address or phone number above.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information. Our website is served over HTTPS and contact form submissions are transmitted securely.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised. Continued use of our website after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
