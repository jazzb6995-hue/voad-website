What We Do

Architecture & Interior Design in Rajkot.
Six Specialisms. One Integrated Studio.

From the first sketch to the final handover, VOAD offers complete architectural and interior design services tailored to each client's vision: residential, commercial, or heritage.

01

Residential Architecture

Bespoke homes designed around how people actually live, including bungalows, villas, and farmhouses tailored to the Gujarat climate and your family's lifestyle. Every plan begins with understanding how you want to inhabit space.

02

Interior Design

Refined interiors for residences, kitchens, suites, and premium hospitality environments. We work with materials, light, and proportion to create spaces that feel both sophisticated and deeply personal.

03

Commercial & Office Design

Workspaces designed to support productivity, reflect brand identity, and attract talent. From boutique studios to corporate offices, we design environments that communicate who you are before a word is spoken.

04

Heritage Designs

Sympathetic restoration and adaptive reuse of historic havelis and heritage buildings. We preserve original craftsmanship: carved jharokhas, lime plaster, and stone floors, while bringing spaces into contemporary use.

05

Renovation & Remodeling

Transforming existing spaces through improved layouts, upgraded materials, and refined finishes. Whether a single room or an entire floor, we approach renovation with the same rigour as new construction.

06

Turnkey Solutions

Complete project execution from concept development through construction supervision to final handover. One point of contact, full accountability, so you can focus on your life while we build your space.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does VOAD Architecture & Interiors offer in Rajkot?+

VOAD offers six core services from its Rajkot studio: Residential Architecture (bungalows, villas, farmhouses), Interior Design (luxury residences and hospitality), Commercial and Office Design, Heritage Designs (haveli restoration), Renovation and Remodelling, and complete Turnkey Solutions with delivery from concept to handover.

Who is the architect at VOAD in Rajkot?+

VOAD is led by Ar. Vivek Bosmiya, Principal Architect and Interior Designer based in Rajkot, Gujarat. He has nine years of architectural practice and has completed over 70 projects across Gujarat and India, personally managing every project from brief to handover.

Does VOAD take on projects outside Rajkot?+

Yes. Our studio is based in Rajkot and we know Gujarat's building culture, materials, and craft traditions deeply. We have worked with clients in Ahmedabad, Surat, Vadodara, Jamnagar, and across Gujarat, and we travel to site for every project wherever it is in India. If the brief is interesting and the fit is right, location is not a constraint.

Which cities does VOAD serve across Gujarat and India?+

We are headquartered in Rajkot and work regularly across Gujarat including Ahmedabad, Surat, Vadodara, Gandhinagar, Jamnagar, and Bhavnagar. We also take on architecture and interior design projects in Mumbai, Pune, and other cities across India. Ar. Vivek Bosmiya travels to site personally for every project, so geography does not limit who we can work with.

What is VOAD's architecture and interior design process?+

VOAD follows a five-stage process: Brief and Discovery (understanding your lifestyle and aspirations), Concept Design (layouts and mood boards), Design Development (full drawings and material specifications), Construction Supervision (on-site coordination), and Handover (walk-through and documentation). Ar. Vivek Bosmiya remains your single point of contact throughout.
