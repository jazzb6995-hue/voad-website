What Drives Us

Our Core Values

These principles guide every decision we make, from the scale of a material choice to the structure of a project timeline.

■

Listening Before Drawing

Every project begins with understanding your lifestyle, your aspirations, your constraints. We do not begin designing until we are certain we have heard what matters most.

□

Detail as a Discipline

The difference between good and exceptional is almost always in the details. We give the same attention to a door handle profile as to a structural column, because our clients will live with both.

△

Honest About Timelines

We set realistic schedules and keep to them. If something changes, you hear about it from us directly, before it becomes a problem.

○

Material Integrity

We recommend materials for their suitability, durability, and authenticity, not their margin. Local stone, handcrafted joinery, and honest finishes are always preferred over the decorative shortcut.

◇

Rooted in Context

Architecture should belong to its place. We design with the Gujarat climate, craft traditions, and local building culture in mind, creating spaces that feel native to their setting.

◆

One Studio Accountable

You work with Ar. Vivek Bosmiya from start to finish. No hand-offs to junior staff, no diluted attention. Full accountability, one point of contact throughout.

Common Questions

About the Studio

What credentials does Ar. Vivek Bosmiya hold?+

Ar. Vivek Bosmiya holds a Bachelor of Architecture degree and is a registered member of the Council of Architecture, India. He has nine years of professional practice and has personally managed over 70 projects across Gujarat and India, spanning residential architecture, luxury interior design, commercial fitouts, and heritage restoration.

Does VOAD handle turnkey interior projects?+

Yes. Turnkey delivery is one of our core services. We manage the project from design concept through to handover, coordinating contractors, vendors, material procurement, and on-site supervision so the client does not have to manage multiple parties. The result is a single point of accountability and a delivered space rather than a set of drawings. This works particularly well for clients who do not have time to manage a project themselves or who are commissioning from another city.

What types of projects does VOAD specialise in?+

VOAD works across six categories: Residential Architecture (bungalows, villas, farmhouses), Interior Design (luxury homes and hospitality), Commercial and Office Design, Heritage Designs (haveli restoration and conservation), Renovation and Remodelling, and Turnkey Solutions. Our strongest work tends to be in bespoke residences and heritage-sensitive projects, where the design demands a high degree of craft and site-specific thinking.

Why choose a smaller studio over a larger firm?+

In a larger architecture or interior design firm, your project is often handed to a junior team after the first meeting. The principal you met becomes a distant figure who reviews work rather than creates it. At VOAD, Ar. Vivek Bosmiya leads the design thinking and remains your primary contact from brief to handover. You get senior-level attention throughout, not just at the pitch stage. For clients who want the person they hired to actually be on their project, a studio like VOAD is the better choice.

Does the studio take on projects across India?+

Yes. While our roots are in Rajkot and most of our work is across Gujarat, including Ahmedabad, Surat, Vadodara, Jamnagar, and Bhavnagar, we do take on projects in other parts of India when the brief is the right fit. We have completed residential interior design work in Mumbai and Pune. For projects farther away, Ar. Vivek Bosmiya travels to site at key stages and manages coordination remotely between visits. If you are based outside Gujarat and considering VOAD, reach out and we will be honest about whether it makes sense.
